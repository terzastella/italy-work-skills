# Calendar format mix

Typical week (3-6 pieces): 1 guide + 1-2 socials + 1 biweekly newsletter.
Balance: value 40% · product 30% · social proof 20% · behind the scenes 10%.
Rotating formats: guide, checklist, case, common mistake, audience question, update.
Every piece reusable: 1 blog → 3 socials + 1 newsletter (see repurpose-content).
