# Gap comparison method

1. List your titles/URLs (max 30, the main ones).
2. List 2-3 competitors with their titles (from sitemaps/blogs).
3. Normalize themes (same topic, different titles = same theme).
4. Table: Theme | You | Comp.A | Comp.B | Gap? (yes = only them).
5. Priority: strong intent × low effort × possible angle.
6. Per top gap: format + angle + CTA.
