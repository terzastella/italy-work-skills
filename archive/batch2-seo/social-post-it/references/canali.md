# social-post-it channel rules

| Channel | Length | Hashtags | Tone |
|---------|--------|----------|------|
| LinkedIn | ≤150 words, short paragraphs | 3-5 niche | professional direct |
| Instagram | hook + ≤80 words | 5 mix popular/niche | visual, first line carries |
| X | ≤280 chars | 1-2 | terse |
| Facebook | ≤100 words | 2-3 | sober conversational |

Carousel: slide 1 hook, slides 2-6 one point each, last CTA. Max 8 slides.
Video: 3-second written hook + 3 points + CTA.
