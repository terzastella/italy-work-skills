# Headline patterns

- Direct: `<benefit> in <time>` e.g. "Excel budget in 10 minutes"
- Number: `<n> ways/mistakes/steps to <goal>`
- Question: `<question the reader asks>?`
- Emotional: `<result> without <pain>` e.g. without losing your mind
- Guide: `<how> + <result>` e.g. "How to clean a messy CSV"

Banned: empty superlatives, unkeepable promises, invented numbers.
Each headline ≤70 chars, count shown.
