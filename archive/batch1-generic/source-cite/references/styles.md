# source-cite styles

One style per document, your pick:

- **Simple** (default): `[1] Author. Title. Publisher, date. <link>` + `Supports:` line
- **Academic**: `Author (year). Title. Publisher. <link>`
- **Link+pages**: for PDFs/books with exact cited pages

Tags: `[weak]` fragile source · `[secondary]` quote of a quote ·
`[offline]` no link · `[source unverified]` never with invented link.
