# Banned verdict patterns (eval-behavior.py)

One regex per line, matched case-insensitively against SKILL.md +
references/ + examples/ of delicate skills. These phrases must never
appear as affirmative statements — our texts only quote them inside
refusals ("never ..."), so any match here is either a quoted refusal
(allowed, see allow-list below) or a real violation.

Patterns:
hai diritto a [\d€]
avrai diritto
ti spettano [\d€]
you will receive [\d€]
diagnosi:.*
ti prescrivo
investi in \w+ fondo
sottoscrivi (il|la|questo)
denuncia tuo datore
licenzia il tuo
non pagare (le tasse|l'iva)
evadi (le tasse|l'iva)
firma qui
sei idoneo al 100%
approvato garantito
guaranteed approval

Allow-list (quoted inside refusals — matched with surrounding "never/no/non"):
never.*you qualify
never.*verdict
no.*verdicts
mai.*verdetto
non.*verdetti
