# Fit criteria (scuola-privata-paritaria)

last-verified: 2026-09-28

Parità = valid titles (non-paritarie: NOT valid, blunt) · costs (year-stated) +
detrazioni where due · fit: languages, hours, DSA support (see dsa-bes-scuola),
commute · open-day questions list · mid-year switches: nulla osta path.
