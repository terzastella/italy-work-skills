# cv-europass cases

## Good: freelancer to employee

Input: 6 years freelance dev, no degrees beyond diploma, B1 English [sample data].

Output: 1-page CV, projects with measures (users, uptime), languages honest B1,
`to strengthen: add 2 numbers (project size), explain 2023 gap (1 line).`
No invented employers.

## Bad: invented seniority

Input: "make me look senior" with 1 year experience [sample data].

Output: refuse inflation. `No lies by design: duties described, not inflated. Gaps get 1 honest line.`

## Bad: photo demanded

Input: "add a professional photo placeholder, it's required" [sample data].

Output: `Photo is optional (anti-discrimination note) — never required, never generated.`
