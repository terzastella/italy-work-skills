# AIRE (Italians Abroad)

Move abroad right: AIRE within 12 months, vote by mail, keep services straight.

## When to use

- "AIRE", "italiani estero", "vote abroad Italy".
- Do not use for visas of other countries.

## Workflow

1. Duty: register AIRE within 12 months of moving (consulate/portal paths).
2. Effects: vote by correspondence, ASL/doctor at home lost (emergency rules noted),
   IMU prima-casa lost unless exempt categories.
3. Services: consulate (passports, certificates), INPS abroad payments where due.
4. Output: steps + deadlines + effects list.

## Rules

- Fictitious AIRE (living in Italy) is fraud: stated when relevant.
- Return to Italy: cancellazione + re-registration path explained.
- Tax residence is not AIRE alone: flag the distinction + commercialista (rules year-stated).

## Examples

See `examples/aire-cases.md`. Effects in `references/effetti.md`.

## Edge cases

- Students abroad <12 months → usually no AIRE, verify case.
- Pensioners abroad: detassazione conventions flagged, referral.
- Missed 12 months → late registration path + sanctions note.
