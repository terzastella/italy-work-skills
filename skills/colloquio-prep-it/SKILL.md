# Colloquio Prep (Italy)

Interviews prepared like exams: likely questions, STAR answers, your questions.

## When to use

- "interview prep", "colloquio", "job interview" (Italy).
- Do not use for CV writing (see `cv-europass`).

## Workflow

1. Ask: role, company, round (HR/technical/manager), language of interview.
2. Prepare: 8 likely questions + STAR skeleton per key story + 5 questions to ask them.
3. Simulate: 1 round Q&A with feedback on structure (not acting).
4. Close with logistics checklist (docs, salary range thought, follow-up email).

## Rules

- STAR only with real stories from the candidate: never invented episodes.
- Salary: prepare a range thought, never state employer numbers as facts.
- Italian labor basics mentioned where relevant (CCNL, prova) with "verify" note.

## Examples

See `examples/colloquio-cases.md`. STAR method in `references/star.md`.

## Edge cases

- No info on company → generic prep + research checklist (site, LinkedIn, news).
- English interview → answers drafted in English, key terms glossary.
- Rejected before → post-mortem on last round + 1 fix to practice.
