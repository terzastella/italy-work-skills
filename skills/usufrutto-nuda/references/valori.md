# Usufruct values (usufrutto-nuda)

last-verified: 2026-09-28

Usufruttuario: use + fruits · nudo proprietario: bare title now, full later.
Usufrutto % by age bands (year-stated) → nuda = remainder. Costs split:
IMU/ordinary to usufruttuario, extraordinary to nudo (standard split, verify deed).
Sale of either share preserves the other. Notary owns deeds.
