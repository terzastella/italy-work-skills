# Buoni Fruttiferi Postali

Postal bonds decoded: types, net returns, early exit, guarantee.

## When to use

- "buoni fruttiferi", "postal bonds Italy".
- Do not use for investment advice (comparison math only).

## Workflow

1. Types: ordinary, 3x2/3x4-style step-ups, dedicated (minors) — current lineup (year-stated).
2. Net math: gross − 12.5% withholding (year-stated) + stamp duty note.
3. Early exit: refundable anytime + accrued rules, no penalties beyond lost uplift.
4. State guarantee backing them (year-stated). No product endorsement.

## Rules

- Rates with year and series; old series differ — identify first.
- Net as verdict number, never gross.
- No "better than X" verdicts: math + questions only.

## Examples

See `examples/buoni-cases.md`. Net math in `references/netto.md`.

## Edge cases

- Lost paper buoni → duplicate/amortization path + Poste office.
- Heirs cashing → succession documents needed, flag + referral.
- Dematerialized vs paper: same rights, different handling noted.
