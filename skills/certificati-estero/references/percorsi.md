# Legalization paths (certificati-estero)

last-verified: 2026-09-28

Hague country: apostille at competent authority (prefettura for civil acts,
procura for judicial) · non-Hague: full chain (prefettura + MFA + consulate) ·
sworn translation via tribunale asseverazione where needed · timeliness: fresh
certificates usually required · fees with year. Verify destination status live.
