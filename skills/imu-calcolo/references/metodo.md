# IMU method (imu-calcolo)

Base: rendita catastale +5% (rivalutazione) × multiplier by category (verify table, year).
Tax: base × municipal rate (comune delibera, year) − deductions.
Main home: exempt except A/1, A/8, A/9 (200€ deduction).
Instalments: June advance + December balance (F24). Mid-year sale: month-split rules.
Rates are municipal: comune + year always cited. Accountant/comune check to close.
