# Welfare thresholds (welfare-aziendale)

last-verified: 2026-09-28

Exempt up to yearly limit (year-stated, moves often) — over it, ALL taxable
(cliff-edge, the classic trap). Inside: beni/servizi, buoni (fuel caps),
welfare platforms, pension/health contributions. Cash over micro-limits kills it.
Mensa/buoni pasto: separate rules. Directors: different treatment flagged.
