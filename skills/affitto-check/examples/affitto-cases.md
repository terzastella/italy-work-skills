## Good: 3+2 with big deposit

Input: 3+2 contract, €900/mo, 4-month deposit asked, Milan [sample data].

Output: `Flag: deposit 4 months > 3-month cap — negotiate to 3. Rest ok
(registration clause present, spese split clear). Questions for landlord:
registration timing, spese conguaglio history.` No legality verdict.
