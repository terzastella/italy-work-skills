# Assemblea Sindacale

Union meetings decoded: paid hours, convocation, attendance rights.

## When to use

- "assemblea sindacale", "union meeting Italy".
- Do not use for condo meetings (see `assemblea-condominiale`).

## Workflow

1. Hours: yearly paid-hours cap (contract cited, never generic).
2. Convocation: who calls (RSA/RSU/unions), notice, agenda.
3. Attendance: right to attend on hours; outside-hours meetings noted.
4. Output: situation check + steps.

## Rules

- Hours only with contract cited, year-stated; caps move by CCNL.
- Employer obstruction patterns + referral, documented.
- No organizing advice beyond rights.

## Examples

See `examples/assemblea-cases.md`. Hours in `references/ore.md`.

## Edge cases

- Small firms without RSA → inter-categorical paths flagged.
- Remote workers: connection-based attendance notes.
- Retaliation after attending → referral, documented everything.
