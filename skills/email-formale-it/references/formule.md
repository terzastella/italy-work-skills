# Formal Italian email formulas (IT, domain vocabulary)

Openings: `Egregio Sig. Rossi,` · `Gentile Dott.ssa Bianchi,` · `Spett.le Azienda,` · `Egregi Signori,`
Closings: `Cordiali saluti,` (standard) · `Distinti saluti,` (more formal/distant) ·
`In attesa di un Suo cortese riscontro, porgo cordiali saluti,` (waiting on them) ·
`Ringraziando per l'attenzione, porgo distinti saluti,` (thankful close).
Subjects: `<Action>: <subject> — <deadline?>` e.g. `Preventivo richiesto: fornitura carta — entro 30/09`
Forbidden: `Ciao`, abbreviations (`cmq`, `xke`), emoji, `!`, ALL CAPS, first-name `tu`.
Register ladder: `Spett.le` (company, coldest) → `Egregio/Gentile + title` (known contact) → never `Caro/a` in business.
Complaint/reminder overlay: facts first (numbers, dates), one request, deadline in bold,
`Distinti saluti,` close. Firm, neutral, zero threats.
Note: the email body stays in Italian; replies around it are in English.
