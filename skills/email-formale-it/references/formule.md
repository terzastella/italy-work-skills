# Formal Italian email formulas (IT, domain vocabulary)

Openings: `Egregio Sig. Rossi,` · `Gentile Dott.ssa Bianchi,` · `Spett.le Azienda,`
Closings: `Cordiali saluti,` · `Distinti saluti,` (more formal) · `In attesa di riscontro, porgo cordiali saluti,`
Subjects: `<Action>: <subject> — <deadline?>` e.g. `Preventivo richiesto: fornitura carta — entro 30/09`
Forbidden: `Ciao`, abbreviations, emoji, `!`, all caps.
Note: the email body stays in Italian; replies around it are in English.
