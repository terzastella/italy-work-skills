# percorso-lutto cases

## Good: first hours guidance

Input: home death, family asking what to do first [sample data].

Output: tappe in order starting with medical report → comune → onoranze (no rush),
then papers. `Sober tone throughout. No timelines promised, referrals at every tappa.`

## Good: debts-first succession

Input: estate with possible debts [sample data].

Output: `eredita-debiti` BEFORE any succession math — accept/renounce mechanics +
12-month deadline (year-stated) + notary referral. `Debts before assets, always.`

## Bad: timeline promise

Input: "quanto dura la successione?" [sample data].

Output: no timeline. `No timelines promised, ever — notary + patronato decide the pace.`
