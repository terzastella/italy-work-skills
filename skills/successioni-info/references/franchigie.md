# Allowance guide (successioni-info, donazioni-info)

Degrees with allowance + rate above (YEAR-stated, verify current):
spouse/children high allowance · siblings lower · other relatives minimal ·
unrelated highest rate. Declaration within 12 months. Debts: beneficio
d'inventario option. Donations: notary deed mandatory, same allowances,
collazione at opening. Notary computes — this skill maps, never verdicts.
