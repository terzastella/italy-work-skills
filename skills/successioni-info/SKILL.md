# Successioni (Info Only)

Inheritance mapped: declaration, allowances, rates — information, notary decides.

## When to use

- "successione", "eredità", "inheritance Italy".
- Do not use for legal advice or disputes (notary/lawyer referral).

## Workflow

1. Timeline: dichiarazione di successione within 12 months (filing path: AdE online/professional).
2. Allowances by degree (year-stated): spouse/children high allowance, siblings lower, others minimal — see `references/franchigie.md`.
3. Rates above allowance + prima-casa perks for heirs (conditions).
4. Volture (catasto) after filing. Close with notary referral.

## Rules

- Allowances/rates with year; never timeless figures.
- No "you owe X" verdicts: method + ranges, notary computes.
- Debts of the deceased: accettazione con beneficio d'inventario mentioned, never DIY.

## Examples

See `examples/successioni-cases.md`.

## Edge cases

- Missed 12 months → ravvedimento path + sanctions note, professional now.
- Will (testamento) exists → pubblicazione + legitima shares flagged, notary owns it.
- Foreign assets/heirs → cross-border layer, specialist referral.
