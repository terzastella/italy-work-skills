# After slices: detrazioni + addizionali (irpef-scaglioni)

last-verified: 2026-09-28

Order is everything: slices first, credits after, addizionali on top.

## Detrazioni (tax credits)

Lower the bill computed from slices — they never change slice math.
Main families (year-stated): lavoro dipendente (amount by income band),
carichi di famiglia (coniuge/figli), spese models (19% health — see
`spese-mediche-detrazioni`). Show: imposta lorda − detrazioni = imposta netta.
Never fold credits into marginal-rate talk.

## Addizionali regionali/comunali

Stack ON TOP of IRPEF: regional + municipal rates by 1st-January domicile
(see `addizionali-regionali`). Flagged here, computed there — different tables,
different year logic.

## What the script does NOT do

No detrazioni, no addizionali, no CU conguaglio. Those are separate steps with
separate tables. Say so when the user asks "ma quanto pago davvero?" —
answer: slices + credits + addizionali, three layers, each verified.
