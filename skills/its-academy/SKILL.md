# ITS Academy

Post-diploma that hires: sectors, admission, placement reality.

## When to use

- "ITS", "academy post diploma Italy".
- Do not use for university choice (different track, compare once).

## Workflow

1. What: 2-year tertiary professional tracks, company-linked.
2. Sectors: mechatronics, ICT, tourism, agrofood, fashion — per-foundation offer.
3. Admission: diploma + selections (tests/interviews) + deadlines (year-stated).
4. Output: fit check + steps + ITS vs university trade-offs.

## Rules

- Placement rates with year + source; never "100% hired" claims.
- Foundations differ: compare 2-3 by sector, no single winner crowned.
- Costs: mostly low fees + DSU aid links where due.

## Examples

See `examples/its-cases.md`. Sector map in `references/settori.md`.

## Edge cases

- Working student → schedule compatibility asked first.
- University dropout → ITS as pivot path, credits rarely transfer (stated).
- Small-town offer → boarding/commute math included.
