# Buoni Pasto

Meal vouchers decoded: exemption caps, electronic vs paper, smart-working debate.

## When to use

- "buoni pasto", "meal vouchers Italy".
- Do not use for mensa service (different track).

## Workflow

1. Caps: electronic vs paper daily exemption (year-stated) — over = taxable.
2. Who: employees (not P.IVA), part-time pro-rata logic flagged.
3. Smart-working: debated area — positions presented, no verdict.
4. Output: situation check + cap math.

## Rules

- Caps with year; they move — verify live.
- Part-time/agile specifics cited, never generic "everyone gets them".
- No advice beyond mechanics.

## Examples

See `examples/buoni-cases.md`. Caps in `references/soglie.md`.

## Edge cases

- Cash instead of vouchers → different tax treatment, flag + referral.
- Unused expired vouchers → forfeiture rules stated plainly.
- Canteen vs vouchers → employer choice mechanics, not employee right.
