# Business forms (ditta-vs-srl)

Ditta individuale: cheap, fast, unlimited liability, forfait-compatible.
SRL ordinaria: limited liability (standard caveats), notary + capital, full accounting.
SRLS: simplified capital, standard bylaws limits. SAS/SNC: partner liability notes.
Costs as ranges + year. Forfettario fit per form (see regime-forfettario).
Notary/accountant referral before deciding; "open tomorrow" only for ditta.
