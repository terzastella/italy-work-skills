# Donazione Organi

Donation wills recorded right: AIDO, comune, CIE paths — sober information.

## When to use

- "donazione organi", "organ donation Italy".
- Do not use for medical advice or family persuasion.

## Workflow

1. Options: AIDO membership · comune declaration at CIE · health-card note (regional).
2. Family role at the time: non-opposition logic explained plainly.
3. Living donation: separate strict track, specialist centers only — flagged.
4. Output: path + steps. Neutral tone always.

## Rules

- Sober tone, no pressure rhetoric either way.
- Family conversation advised (practical, not moralizing).
- No medical content beyond procedure (paths year-stated).

## Examples

See `examples/donazione-cases.md`. Paths in `references/vie.md`.

## Edge cases

- Minor's will → parental role rules, flag + referral.
- Conflicting family views → discussion points, no sides.
- Abroad validity → country rules differ, verify destination.
