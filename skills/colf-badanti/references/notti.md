# Night shifts (colf-badanti)

The most misunderstood corner of domestic work. Year-stated, CCNL-cited.

## Presence vs active hours

Presence (being there, sleeping): compensated as availability with its rules.
Active hours (waking, working): paid as work. The contract states which applies
when — read it, never assume night = flat rate.

## Convivente nights

Night presence is part of the convivente deal structure, with limits and
compensations per CCNL table (year-stated). Beyond limits → overtime logic.

## Red flags

Night-only hiring without day terms, no rest days, on-call 24/7 expectations:
mismatch list + referral. Rest is a right, nights included.
