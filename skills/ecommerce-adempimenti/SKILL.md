# Ecommerce Adempimenti

Sell online legally: SCIA, invoices, returns, privacy — the full map.

## When to use

- "vendere online", "ecommerce adempimenti", "open online shop Italy".
- Do not use for marketing (traffic/pricing out of scope).

## Workflow

1. Start: SCIA/comunicazione to comune + chamber registration (see `camera-commercio`).
2. Sell: e-invoices vs corrispettivi telematici for online, EU OSS for cross-border B2C.
3. Consumer duties: withdrawal info, terms, privacy/GDPR minimum (see `privacy-informativa`).
4. Output: step map + costs + professional referral.

## Rules

- Occasional sales ≠ business: habituality test first (see collaborazioni-occasionali).
- EU B2C: OSS scheme flagged early, not discovered at fine time (rules year-stated).
- Platform (marketplace) vs own shop: different duty mixes, mapped separately.

## Examples

See `examples/ecommerce-cases.md`. Step map in `references/mappa.md`.

## Edge cases

- Dropshipping: supplier/returns/VAT chain explained + extra caution.
- Digital products: VAT-place rules differ, referral.
- Food/cosmetics/special goods: sector authorizations flagged, never generalized.
