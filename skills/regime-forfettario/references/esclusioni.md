# Exclusion causes (regime-forfettario) — check every one, yearly

The gates in `requisiti.md` say who fits; this file says who is OUT no matter
the revenue. One hit = out. Verify against current law text, never memory.

## Hard exclusions (any one kills access)

- Special VAT regimes (agricoltura, editoria-margin, travel-agency-margin, etc.).
- Prior-employer prevalence: main client = ex-employer of the last 2 years
  (the silent killer — ask employment history at tappa 1, always).
- Employee/pension income over the yearly limit (35.000€ for 2026 → 30.000€
  in 2027 per current law; exceptions: ended job, arrears — verify).
- Exceeding 100.000€ takings/accrual in the year: immediate exit.
- Property sales under exempt regimes where due (niche, flag + referral).
- E-invoicing refusal: mandatory, no opt-out.

## Soft checks (route, don't verdict)

- Mixed professional/firm income → which threshold base applies (takings vs accrual).
- Foreign clients majority → reverse-charge interplay (see `operazioni-estero`),
  forfait stays possible, filing differs.
- Second activity with different ATECO → coefficients per activity, revenue summed.

## Output contract

List each cause as PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN from user facts. UNKNOWN blocks any
"likely eligible" language — unknowns first, math second, accountant last.
