# Bagagli Smarriti

Lost bags recovered or paid: PIR now, Montreal limits, 21-day clock.

## When to use

- "bagaglio smarrito", "lost luggage Italy".
- Do not use for flight delays (see `voli-ritardi`, `treni-diritti`).

## Workflow

1. PIR immediately at arrival airport (before leaving) + photos of tags.
2. 21-day search window: interim expenses (receipts kept) reimbursable logic.
3. Lost for good: Montreal Convention cap (DSP, year-stated) + contents list with values.
4. Output: steps + deadlines + claim draft.

## Rules

- PIR timing stressed first: late reports weaken everything.
- Valuables/electronics limits noted (special declaration option mentioned).
- No outcome promises.

## Examples

See `examples/bagagli-cases.md`. Limits in `references/limiti.md`.

## Edge cases

- Damaged (not lost) → 7-day written notice rule, stated first.
- Low-cost no-desk chaos → online PIR + timestamps kept as proof.
- Home airline vs operating carrier → liable party identified correctly.
