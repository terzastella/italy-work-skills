# Reading grid (bandi-pmi)

Extract in order: 1) eligible subjects (ATECO/size/region) 2) fundable vs excluded costs
3) aid intensity % + de minimis note 4) deadlines (submit + spend + report) 5) scoring weights
6) mandatory attachments 7) info desk contacts.

Go/no-go (all five must lean yes):
- Eligible? (article cited — one wrong ATECO digit kills it)
- Costs fit? (fundable share of YOUR budget, excluded costs listed)
- Deadline feasible? (quotes + attachments lead time vs closing date/time/timezone)
- Realistic score? (weights × your strengths, cutoff history if published)
- Paperwork load? (reporting burden vs grant size — small grants can cost more than they pay)

Verdict + first thing to prepare + official link. Never promise winning.
Expired → say expired first, then replacement edition. Vague text → ambiguities
listed + where to ask (info desk, call FAQ). Regional+national overlap → compare,
pick one (cumulation rules, accountant for de minimis).
